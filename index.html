About Shivam

I am Shivam Kumar, a Medical Laboratory Technology graduate from Sanskaram University, with a strong interest in laboratory science, diagnostics, hematology, genetics, and the role of science in improving patient care.

My Story

My journey in Medical Laboratory Technology is driven by curiosity, continuous learning, and a desire to understand how laboratory investigations help healthcare professionals make informed decisions. I am especially drawn to the science behind blood, cells, genes, and disease processes.

My Favourite Subjects

Hematology & Genetics

Two areas that especially interest me because they connect laboratory investigation with the understanding of blood, cells, chromosomes, genes, inheritance, and disease.

🩸 Hematology

I enjoy learning about blood cells, hemoglobin, blood disorders, peripheral blood smears, hematological investigations, and the laboratory principles used to evaluate blood-related conditions.

🧬 Genetics

I am interested in genes, chromosomes, inheritance, genetic variation, mutations, and the laboratory concepts behind genetic testing and molecular investigation.
